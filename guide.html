Leonida Legend — GTA VI Trophy Guide

All 55 trophies: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze. No online trophies. About 200 hours, difficulty 4/10. Global release 19 November 2026.

Progress: 0 / 55 unlocked. Hidden: 28. Unconfirmed: 5. Missable: 1 (Positive Mental Attitude, Chapter 1).

Platinum roadmap

  1. Phase 1 — Story (~60h): all 5 chapters, character finales, meditation spots in Chapter 1, rotate the 15 manual saves.
  2. Phase 2 — Side content (~80h): strangers, cards, photos, vehicles, garages, drone races, fishing, arcade, max Strength.
  3. Phase 3 — Grind and 100% (~60h): stunts, shooting-range Golds, races, vaults, gang operations, laundering, challenges, scans.

100% checklist

Vehicles 200/277 · Animals 100/255 · Challenges 70/80 · Stunts 300 · Optional missions 100.
