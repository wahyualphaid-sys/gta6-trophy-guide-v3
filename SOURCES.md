# SOURCES.md — GTA VI trophy data

All trophy names, grades, Gamerscore values, descriptions and unlock routes in
this guide were verified against live sources on **19–20 November 2026**.
Nothing here is copied from another guide's prose; the wording is original,
and the facts were cross-checked between sources before being written down.

## Primary sources

1. **PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap**
   https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-grand-theft-auto-vi-trophy-guide-roadmap/
   Used for: the full 55-trophy list, grades, Gamerscore, story chapter
   structure (5 chapters), the 100% completion checklist, and the missable
   warning for Positive Mental Attitude (Chapter 1).

2. **Output Lag — GTA 6 Trophy List**
   https://outputlag.com/guides/gta-6-trophy-list/
   Used for: cross-checking every trophy name, grade and description,
   including the hidden story trophies and the five trophies with no
   confirmed method.

## Cross-checks

- **KeenGamer** and **GTABase** trophy listings, checked 19–20 November 2026:
  both agree with PowerPyx and Output Lag on the 55-trophy count
  (1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze), the chapter trophies, and
  the collectible / activity counts (250 stunts, 42 shooting-range
  challenges, 14 drone-race locations, 18 meditation spots, 9 gangs,
  8 garages, 3 card sets, 100 WAiNK spots).

## Facts established by those sources

- Global release: 19 November 2026.
- 5 chapters; no mission replay; 15 manual save slots.
- 0 online trophies.
- Cheats disable trophies.
- Estimated Platinum: ~200 hours, difficulty about 4/10.
- 100% checklist: 200/277 vehicles scanned, 100/255 animals studied,
  70/80 challenges, 300 stunts, 100 optional missions.
- One potentially missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude (Chapter 1).

## What is NOT verified — flagged, never invented

Five hidden Bronze trophies have no confirmed unlock method in any source
checked (PowerPyx, Output Lag, KeenGamer, GTABase, 19–20 November 2026):

- In The Beginning… — "Enjoy a cocktail with a view on Ocean Beach"
- Capital Offense — "Smash your way out of a vehicle showroom"
- Bad Luck, I Guess — "Second time's the charm"
- The Truth Is Out There — "What the hell just happened?"
- Ten Pin — "Bowl 'em over with a melee drive-by"

They are listed with their official descriptions only, marked UNCONFIRMED in
`main.ts`, `guide.md` and `guide.html`. No method is given for them anywhere
in this repository. If a later source verifies a method, update the entry
and note the source here.
