## Platinum roadmap (3 phases, ~200 hours)

### Phase 1 — Story first, clean up as you go (~60 hours)
Play all 5 chapters, finish every story mission and the four character finale strands (Kenton, Billy, Andy, Randy, Tyrone). Do Positive Mental Attitude meditation spots in Chapter 1 while you can. Keep 15 manual saves and rotate them before risky missions, because there is no mission replay.

### Phase 2 — Side content and collectibles (~80 hours)
Stranger strands (8), card sets (3), 100 WAiNK photo spots, 100 vehicles discovered, all of Leonida explored, 8 garages bought, 10 vehicles registered, drone races at all 14 locations, fishing and arcade games, both characters to max Strength.

### Phase 3 — Grind and 100% (~60 hours)
250 stunts (300 for 100%), 42 shooting-range Gold medals, 20 different race wins, 5 bank vaults, 9 gang operations, $250,000 laundered, $100,000 spent, 50 challenges (70 for 100%), 200/277 vehicles scanned, 100/255 animals studied, 100 optional missions.
