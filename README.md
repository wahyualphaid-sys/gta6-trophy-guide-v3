An offline, spoiler-safe trophy guide and tracker for **Grand Theft Auto VI** — all **55 trophies**: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze. Global release 19 November 2026. No online trophies. Estimated Platinum time **~200 hours**, difficulty **4/10**.

Run `node main.ts` to build `guide.md` and `guide.html` from the trophy data, and to see your progress. No dependencies, no network, no account needed.

## Quick facts

- **Release:** 19 November 2026, worldwide
- **Story:** 5 chapters, no mission replay — use the 15 manual save slots and rotate them before risky missions
- **Online:** 0 online trophies; everything is earnable solo, offline
- **Cheats:** cheats disable trophies — play clean until the Platinum pops
- **100% completion checklist:** 200/277 vehicles scanned · 100/255 animals studied · 70/80 challenges · 300 stunts · 100 optional missions
- **The one missable trophy:** Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at all 18 spots (10 locations). It is potentially missable in Chapter 1, so do the meditation spots as soon as you can reach them.

## Platinum roadmap — 3 phases, ~200 hours

### Phase 1 — Story first, clean up as you go (~60 hours)

Play all 5 chapters and finish every story mission, including the character finale strands for Kenton, Billy, Andy, Randy and Tyrone. This phase alone earns the chapter trophies (Paradise Lost, T**s Ass Drug Money, Vice City, Baby!, Top Of The World, Ma), the mission trophies (Gone Fishing, Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child's Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory) and the final-mission Gold (You Gotta Know When to Get Off).

Do the meditation spots for Positive Mental Attitude in Chapter 1 while you can. Rotate your 15 manual saves — there is no mission replay, so a bad save can cost hours.

### Phase 2 — Side content and collectibles (~80 hours)

Stranger mission strands (8 for Strangers & Freaks), 3 collectible card sets (Don't Touch My Deck), 100 WAiNK photo spots (You WAiNKer!), 100 vehicles discovered (Whip It Good), all of Leonida explored (The Goodtime State), 8 garages bought (Little Boxes), 10 vehicles registered to the Scooter Bros app (On the Spot), Top Fun drone races at all 14 locations, fishing (beat Cal for Baited My Tackle), an arcade game (Stick Fiddler), and both Jason and Lucia to maximum Strength (Personal Best).

### Phase 3 — Grind and 100% (~60 hours)

250 stunts (Cunning Stunts; 300 for 100%), Gold medals in all 42 Shooting Range challenges (The Shootist), 20 different race wins (Earn Cash, Not Kudos), 5 bank vaults robbed and escaped (Maximum Withdrawal), an Illegal Operation robbed for each of the 9 gangs (Pest Control), $250,000 laundered in Marked Bills (Dime on the Dollar), $100,000 spent across shops (Treat Yo Self), 50 challenges (One Last Thing; 70 for 100%), and the 100% checklist: 200/277 vehicles scanned, 100/255 animals studied, 100 optional missions. Finish with A Long Road (100% completion, Gold) and the Platinum, Vice Got a Price!

## Trophies by category

**Story (hidden, unmissable if you finish the game):** Just Like Paradise (Gone Fishing), Paradise Lost (Chapter 1), T**s Ass Drug Money (Chapter 2), Vice City, Baby! (Chapter 3), Top Of The World, Ma (Chapter 4), You Gotta Know When to Get Off (final mission, Gold), Trust (Breakfast Run), Break the Bank (The Cartel Heist), Family Matters (Child's Play), DWNFALL (Headliners), Hi-Jacked (VCIA Takedown), Snow Storm (Operation Savage Glory), This Was No Boat Accident (Kenton finale), The Lone Rider (Billy finale), Road to Rehabilitation (Andy finale), Supply & Demand (Randy finale), Spin On This! (Tyrone finale), Fair Game (hunt a unique animal for Bret).

**Exploration:** The Goodtime State (explore all of Leonida), Whip It Good (100 vehicles), Catch Me If You Can (steal a docked airliner at VCIA gates E3/E4), Challenging Tank (Rhino tank at Mile Zero Air Station).

**Combat:** The Shootist (Gold in all 42 Shooting Range challenges, Silver), Gunslinger (5 takedowns in one Combat Focus).

**Heists:** Maximum Withdrawal (5 bank vaults, Silver), Retail Therapy (3 shops back-to-back with your partner), Pest Control (one Illegal Operation per gang, 9 gangs), Sneakthief (jewelry store, undetected), A Classic Blitz Play (Armored Truck).

**Driving:** Cunning Stunts (250 stunts, Silver), Earn Cash, Not Kudos (20 different race wins), On the Spot (10 vehicles registered).

**Activities:** Master of None (try all 9 activity types), Top Fun (drone race win at all 14 locations), Baited My Tackle (beat Cal fishing), Stick Fiddler (complete an arcade game), Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at all 18 spots — **potentially missable, Chapter 1**), Let's Go Bowling (Kolt & Associates bowling alley), Personal Best (both characters max Strength).

**Collectibles:** Don't Touch My Deck (3 card sets, Silver), You WAiNKer! (100 photo spots, Silver).

**Money:** Dime on the Dollar ($250,000 laundered), Treat Yo Self ($100,000 spent), Little Boxes (8 garages).

**Completion:** A Long Road (100%, Gold), One Last Thing (50 challenges), Actions Speak Louder (500 actions that change your Criminal Profile), Hot Together (lose a 4-star Wanted Level with your partner).

**⚠️ Spoiler warning:** the hidden trophies above name story missions and characters. The tracker hides hidden-trophy details by default in spirit — read `guide.md` / `guide.html` after the story if you want zero spoilers.

## The 5 UNCONFIRMED trophies — read this before trusting any guide

Five hidden Bronze trophies have **no verified unlock method** as of 20 November 2026, and this guide does not invent one for them:

| Trophy | Description on the list |
|---|---|
| In The Beginning… | Enjoy a cocktail with a view on Ocean Beach |
| Capital Offense | Smash your way out of a vehicle showroom |
| Bad Luck, I Guess | Second time's the charm |
| The Truth Is Out There | What the hell just happened? |
| Ten Pin | Bowl 'em over with a melee drive-by |

If a video or post claims a confirmed method for any of these, check it against the sources below first. They are marked UNCONFIRMED in `main.ts`, `guide.md` and `guide.html` until a source verifies them.

## Use the tracker

```sh
node main.ts                 # build guide.md + guide.html, show progress
node main.ts --list          # list all 55 trophies by grade
node main.ts --grade Gold    # list one grade (Platinum, Gold, Silver, Bronze)
node main.ts --trophy "Bowling"  # show one trophy by partial name
```

Track unlocks in `progress.json` — a plain list of unlocked trophy names, for example:

```json
["Paradise Lost", "Just Like Paradise", "Let's Go Bowling"]
```

Run `node main.ts` again and your count updates in both generated guides. `guide.html` works offline in any browser; `guide.md` is the same guide in plain text.
